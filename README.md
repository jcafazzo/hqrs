# HQRS 846 — Introductory Lecture Review

An adversarial content and visual-design review of the 2026 introductory lecture for **HQRS 846: Human Factors in Health Care**.

The review preserves the lecture's presenter-led TED/Jobs stagecraft while sharpening its central argument:

> People will err. Systems shape whether error is likely, visible, recoverable, or harmful.

The site includes:

- a ranked content and stagecraft critique;
- nine rendered slide redesign studies;
- three alternative narrative structures;
- contemporary clinical examples and primary-source links;
- a reading-to-lecture map; and
- the visual philosophy behind the redesign.
