# Publication note — Service Design

## Classroom build

This deck preserves all 182 original pages in an in-browser inventory while rebuilding the live path around accountable service blueprinting. Original Medly material remains important classroom evidence, but it is not automatically cleared for public redistribution.

## Public boundary

Do not publish `assets/source/` without an asset-by-asset clearance pass. The archive includes brand imagery, proprietary interfaces, internal maps, participant quotations, operational material, and recognizable patients, clinicians, newcomer families, and children. The folder and its render evidence are excluded by the repository `.gitignore` as a technical guard.

For each retained source asset, record:

1. rights owner and source URL or file;
2. classroom and public-use basis;
3. consent and privacy/PHI status;
4. visible attribution;
5. keep, crop, replace, or remove decision.

The deck engine, Altitude Blue treatments, text, and CSS-authored blueprint diagrams can be published separately after source-image dependencies are replaced or cleared.
