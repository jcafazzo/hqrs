## Truth status

Every asset in this ledger is an original generated teaching image. It is hypothetical or conceptual. It is not a photograph of a reported patient, clinician, institution, device, study site, production interface, or source event. Generated images illustrate circumstances, work, and design questions. They do not prove a claim, rate, mechanism, outcome, or intervention effect.

The visible truth label, alternative text, source boundary, and presenter-guide ledger must travel with a copied slide.

## Prompt grammar

The complete faithful prompt for each accepted asset consists of this shared frame, its row-specific brief below, and the shared exclusions.

**Shared frame:** Create a high-quality 16:9 landscape photorealistic editorial or documentary healthcare teaching image. Use natural colour, believable institutional or home light, candid work, coherent anatomy and equipment, restrained real-world imperfection, and useful negative space for native slide copy. Match the sparse photographic stagecraft of the existing Cafazzo presentations. People are participants in adaptation, detection, handoff, and recovery, not weak links or heroes.

**Shared exclusions:** no logo or identifiable institution; no vendor brand; no readable patient record, medicine, metric, screen, or warning; no embedded explanatory text, arrow, or infographic; no crisis theatre, gore, distress spectacle, blame symbolism, corporate pose, advertising smile, futuristic interface, or claim of clinical evidence.

## Inspection protocol and result

1. Opened every accepted output at its original 1672 by 941 size.
2. Checked hands, faces, equipment, cables, screens, containers, room geometry, and work relationships.
3. Rejected any asset with embedded explanatory text, weak clinical plausibility, generic network symbolism, or a mismatch with the existing Cafazzo visual grammar.
4. Integrated accepted assets as full-field slides with visible generated-image truth labels.
5. Rendered every slide at 1600 by 900 and reviewed each generated-image slide full size and again in its module contact sheet.
6. Corrected seven copy-side compositions after contact-sheet review so native text occupied the intended negative space.
7. Replaced three inherited generated assets whose original generation-call provenance was incomplete with newly generated, fully traceable originals.

Final result: 25 accepted assets, 3 documented rejections, 0 unresolved anatomy, equipment, readable-record, crop, or evidence-boundary failures.
