# Presenter guide: Pulse oximetry

## Contract

- **Audience contract:** In ten minutes, learners will treat SpO2 as a designed estimate and identify one system control that makes uncertainty visible or recoverable.
- **Argument:** The use error is not simply believing a monitor. It is building a system that displays an estimate with great confidence, embeds it in hard thresholds, and leaves a clinician to remember every hidden limitation.
- **Runtime:** 9:45 without discussion. The default classroom route is 10:00 with 35 seconds of pod work and two ten-second reports.
- **Transfer question:** What will your system show when the number and the person disagree?

## Run of show

| Time | Slide | Presenter move | Expected room response | Transition |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | 01 | Run the fist, one-finger, two-finger commitment vote. Give no context. | Most rooms split between reassurance and uncertainty. | “Now let us change only the reference measurement.” |
| 0:45 | 02 | Distinguish optical estimate from arterial reference. | Recognition that the same display can cross a clinical boundary. | “How often did that disagreement appear in the study?” |
| 1:35 | 03 | Read the denominators, then the percentages. Stress measurements and retrospective. | Learners see a disparity without being invited into a one-cause story. | “Before assigning a cause, notice how the number is produced.” |
| 2:45 | 04 | Explain only enough physics to show production history. | Light, tissue, sensor and algorithm are seen as one measurement system. | “Now follow that uncertainty downstream.” |
| 3:35 | 05 | Point across the four-node chain. Ask where the user begins. | No clean boundary between technology, context and person. | “Real use supplies conditions the laboratory cannot wish away.” |
| 4:40 | 06 | Reframe motion, perfusion, temperature and fit as foreseeable conditions. | Suggestions about sensing quality, fit support, trends and escalation. | “Regulation can redesign the system before bedside use.” |
| 5:40 | 07 | Give ten seconds to inspect the draft source page. | Representation is recognized as a validation control. | “Let us try the redesign ourselves.” |
| 6:45 | 08 | Give pods 35 seconds and take two ten-second answers. | Strong answers name a trigger and an action at one node. | “The same draft makes proposed test scale inspectable.” |
| 8:10 | 09 | Point out that the artifact is draft and non-binding. | Learners distinguish a proposed control from implemented law. | “Return to the person who must act on the number.” |
| 9:15 | 10 | Deliver the closing line, then pause. | No discussion required. | End.

## Interaction facilitation

Eight pods can take the following nodes: validation, procurement, sensor, application, display, protocol, clinical assessment, and feedback. The visible slide groups these into four larger families so back-row type stays large.

Strong answers usually include:

- evidence requirements by device model and represented pigmentation;
- signal-quality information visually coupled to the displayed value;
- trend and uncertainty context instead of one isolated point;
- a defined response when symptoms and readings conflict;
- feedback from incidents and performance review into procurement.

If a pod proposes another warning banner, ask: “What action becomes easier, and what trigger activates it?”

## Claims to refuse

- “Pulse oximeters are racist” as a complete causal explanation.
- “Race is skin pigmentation.”
- “Melanin is the only established mechanism.”
- “Pulse oximetry is useless.”
- “Every discordant reading needs an arterial blood gas.”
- “The 11.7% result is a current universal device rate.”

Prefer: multiple studies identified inequity in measurement and recognition. Device, calibration, perfusion, physiology, pigmentation measurement and clinical context can interact.

## Classroom and public-use boundary

- Classroom delivery may show the two FDA page captures for source inspection with full attribution and draft status.
- Before public web reuse, confirm the current FDA source and reuse terms, keep the artifact whole enough to preserve context, and do not present the captured page as current law.
- Generated images can travel with the deck only when their visible truth labels and this ledger remain attached.
- The study figures are rebuilt natively from cited values, not reproduced journal graphics.

## Final rehearsal checks

1. Say “measurements” on slide 03.
2. Say “draft and non-binding” on both FDA slides.
3. Never collapse race into pigmentation or one mechanism.
4. Stop at two reports on the ten-minute route. Four reports belong only in the twelve-minute route.
5. End on the design question, not on distrust of the device.
