# Visual philosophy — Cognition and use error

The deck treats the projector as a stage, not a document viewer. One slide has one teaching job. Observation precedes explanation; evidence is given the full field; reveals are paced so learners can commit, notice, normalize, and redesign.

- Queen's Blue provides the stable field and institutional continuity.
- Gold marks a conceptual pivot or the cue that deserves attention.
- Red is reserved for consequence, mismatch, or correction.
- Large typography carries the argument; supporting detail belongs in presenter notes.
- CSS-authored diagrams simplify mechanisms without pretending to be clinical evidence.
- Source artifacts are shown large enough to inspect, then paired with an explicit system response.

The theatrical rhythm is deliberately familiar to the course's voice, but the instructional ethic has changed: the reveal never humiliates the learner. It demonstrates predictable performance under designed conditions.
