# Publication note — Cognition and use error

## Classroom build

This deck preserves all 77 original pages in an in-browser inventory and selectively reuses source artifacts for classroom critique. Its central live argument is original: detection, memory, judgment, and recovery are conditions to design—not reasons to blame a person.

## Public boundary

Do not publish `assets/source/` without an asset-by-asset clearance pass. The archive includes broadcast imagery, election material, book and media excerpts, study stimuli, clinical artifacts, and material depicting people. Academic teaching use does not automatically make unrestricted web publication appropriate. The folder and its render evidence are excluded by the repository `.gitignore` as a technical guard.

For each retained source asset, record:

1. rights owner and source URL or file;
2. classroom and public-use basis;
3. consent and privacy/PHI status;
4. visible attribution;
5. keep, crop, replace, or remove decision.

The deck engine, Altitude Blue treatments, text, and CSS-authored diagrams can be published separately after the source-image dependencies are replaced or cleared.
