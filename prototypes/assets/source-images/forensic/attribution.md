# Forensic Thread source-image attributions

These files are excerpts from publicly available primary-source investigation reports. They are retained here for attributed, non-commercial classroom critique and teaching. Preserve the attribution on or adjacent to any slide that uses an image.

## `hssib-parkinsons-executive-summary-8-of-18.png`

- **Source/owner:** Health Services Safety Investigations Body (HSSIB), *Medication not given: administration of time critical medication in the emergency department* (5 December 2024)
- **Source page:** <https://www.hssib.org.uk/patient-safety-investigations/medication-related-harm/investigation-report/>
- **Designation:** Clean crop of the report's executive-summary patient-safety-event passage, PDF page 3; includes the official account that only 8 of 18 scheduled doses were provided on time
- **Suggested educational use:** Show briefly as the documentary evidence behind the opening numerical reveal. It works well after the audience has seen `18` and before the forensic widening begins.

## `hssib-parkinsons-care-model.png`

- **Source/owner:** Health Services Safety Investigations Body (HSSIB), *Medication not given: administration of time critical medication in the emergency department* (5 December 2024)
- **Source page:** <https://www.hssib.org.uk/patient-safety-investigations/medication-related-harm/investigation-report/>
- **Designation:** Figure A, “The CARe model,” PDF page 41; the report notes that it used the model to identify adaptations and adjustments that bridge misalignments between demand and capacity
- **Suggested educational use:** Use when moving from a linear event account to work-as-imagined/work-as-done and resilience. The figure gives the lecture a direct methodological connection to the source investigation.

## `hssib-temporary-care-corridor-trolleys.jpg`

- **Source/owner:** Health Services Safety Investigations Body (HSSIB), *Patient care in temporary care environments* (8 January 2026)
- **Source page:** <https://www.hssib.org.uk/patient-safety-investigations/patient-care-in-temporary-care-environments/investigation-report/>
- **Designation:** Figure 1, “Patients on trolleys in a corridor being used as a temporary care environment due to overflow in the emergency department,” PDF page 14
- **Suggested educational use:** Documentary replacement for a generated corridor-care image. The frosted glass and visible trolleys make capacity, surveillance, privacy, and dignity legible without showing identifiable patients.

## `hssib-fatigue-reporting-behaviour.png`

- **Source/owner:** Health Services Safety Investigations Body (HSSIB), *The impact of staff fatigue on patient safety* (24 April 2025)
- **Source page:** <https://www.hssib.org.uk/patient-safety-investigations/the-impact-of-staff-fatigue-on-patient-safety/investigation-report/>
- **Designation:** Figure 1, “Factors that influence reporting behaviour” (adapted by HSSIB for accessibility from Shorrock, 2023), PDF page 22
- **Suggested educational use:** Use to show why “report fatigue” is not a sufficient intervention. Reporting behaviour depends on capability, opportunity, motivation, confidentiality, consequences, management behaviour, work conditions, feedback, and reporting-system design.
