Goal criteria

Non-negotiable acceptance checks.

Cognition has one learner transformation: explain detection, memory, judgment and recovery as system conditions.
Service Design has one learner transformation: turn a journey into an accountable operational blueprint.
The main paths fit 60 and 90 minutes with at least a 10% discussion/transition buffer.
All 44–45 learners have a defined interaction role; activities do not require one specialty’s knowledge.
No exact slide is live in two course decks; repeated material is a teaser or callback only.
Every phenomenon or case lands in an explicit design action or test.
Every empirical claim includes a primary/authoritative source, date, denominator or scope and material limitation.
No bias name stands alone as a retrospective root-cause explanation.
Instructional text is at least 30 px at 1600×900; normal teaching copy targets 34–40 px or larger.
Any page students must inspect occupies the full field; maps and blueprints are never teaching thumbnails.
Queen’s Blue is structural, Gold marks pivots and Red marks consequences or correction.
All 77 and 182 original pages remain reachable and classified keep, refresh or park.

Publication boundary

Classroom defensibility is not automatic web clearance.

Cognition cautions

Broadcast Oscars material, ballot and book excerpts, study stimuli, media screenshots, clinical label artifacts and the hospital video still require rights and privacy review before public reuse.

Service cautions

Brand imagery, Medly interfaces and internal maps, patient/clinician quotations, PPE participants, newcomer families and children, and operational pandemic materials need explicit clearance.

Public package rule

The classroom-only source-slide and render folders are excluded in .gitignore. Do not remove that guard until each image has a rights owner, consent status, PHI/persona status, attribution and a keep/replace decision.

Source PDFs

The two original PDFs remain outside the repository package. The HTML inventory references rendered classroom assets locally.
