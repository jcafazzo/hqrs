# Source-image credits

The prototype decks include limited excerpts from their cited primary sources for noncommercial graduate teaching, criticism, review, and discussion. A compact credit remains visible on every slide that uses a source image. Full provenance, direct source links, figure/page designations, cropping disclosures, teaching uses, and claim guardrails are recorded below.

The source images remain the property of their respective owners unless an identified open licence applies. Attribution is not itself a substitute for applicable institutional copyright and fair-dealing policies. The excerpts should remain connected to the educational analysis and should not be redistributed as a standalone image collection.
