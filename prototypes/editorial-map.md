## How the three prototypes divide the material

- **The Forensic Thread** follows one medication journey and repeatedly widens the frame: action, task, team, organization, environment, policy. It borrows the current deck’s progressive-reveal discipline but replaces example accumulation with causal depth.
- **The Museum of Designed Error** inherits the pill-bottle, EpiPen, pump, and AED object theatre. Three new exhibits make the same stagecraft contemporary while adding resilience and measurement equity.
- **Systems Theatre** inherits the attribution-trap opening and turns it into a room-scale exercise. Nine groups make an early causal choice, receive new system evidence, and revise their explanation in public.
